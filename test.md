# DEAD SIGNAL — ORPHEUS-9

> [!info]- PROPERTIES  
> **Salvage Window:** 47 minutes  
> **Payout:** 8,000 Creds  
> **Survival:** Optional

_SALVAGE WINDOW: 47 MINUTES // PAYOUT: 8,000 CREDS // SURVIVAL: OPTIONAL_

The refinery moon **ORPHEUS-9** has been dead for eleven years. Yesterday it transmitted a fresh distress call using the voice of every **scavver** who ever vanished there.

Your employer wants the station's memory core. They neglected to mention that something inside has learned how to broadcast hunger.

> [!protocol] CONTAMINATION PROTOCOL  
> Anyone who listens to the signal without a filter must test **DR12 Presence**. On a failure, mark one contamination and secretly choose a crewmate whose voice you can now hear inside the walls.

## BREACH THE CORPSE

1. **Docking Spine.** The clamps engage without power. A vac-suited corpse scratches into the outer hatch. `DON'T ANSWER`
    
2. **Choir Deck.** Hundreds of severed comm units whisper in perfect unison. Killing the sound requires a **DR14 Presence** test or industrial explosives.
    
3. **Memory Crypt.** The core is wrapped in warm black tendrils. Removing it starts the station's 6-round collapse.
    

## HULL CHOIR

> [!creature] HULL CHOIR // GRIMM INFESTATION  
> A knot of former workers fused into cabling, pressure suits, and one communal mouth.
> 
> **HP** 18 · **MORALE** 10 · **ARMOR** -2
> 
> **BONE ANTENNA** d8 · **SCREAMING FEEDBACK** d4 Presence loss

### SPECIAL TRAITS

**Broadcast Hunger.** The Hull Choir can imitate the voice of anyone who has spoken over the station's communications network.

**Black Tendrils.** A creature struck by the tendrils must succeed on a **DR12 Agility** test or become restrained.

**Communal Mind.** All connected sections of the Choir share the same senses.

> [!warning] CONTAMINATION  
> Anyone who hears the Choir speak without protection must immediately test **Presence**.

## THE MEMORY CORE

The memory core is cold to the touch, despite the heat radiating from the tendrils surrounding it.

> [!important] DO NOT REMOVE  
> Removing the core begins the station's **6-round collapse**.
> 
> Once removed, the core begins broadcasting a signal that can be heard by every living creature aboard the station.

### EXTRACTION

The crew has **47 minutes** before the salvage window closes.

Every failed extraction attempt advances the station's collapse by **1 round**.

- **Success:** The core is extracted safely.
    
- **Failure:** The station reacts violently.
    
- **Critical failure:** The signal broadcasts the voice of one of the crew.
